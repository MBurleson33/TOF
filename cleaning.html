The Process

How We Clean Your Turf

01

Debris Removal

We sweep and remove leaves, twigs, pet waste, and any surface debris that can trap bacteria and cause odors.

02

Brushing

We brush and lift the turf fibers to prevent matting and restore the natural upright look of your turf blades.

03

OxyTurf Application

We apply OxyTurf cleaner throughout the turf, penetrating deep to kill 99.9% of germs and eliminate odors at the source.

04

Final Rinse

A thorough rinse removes all residue, leaving your turf fresh, clean, and safe for immediate use by kids and pets.
