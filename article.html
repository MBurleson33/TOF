The Environmental Advantages of Installing Artificial Turf

TOF Turf + Outdoors · 8 min read · Oklahoma City, OK

What is Artificial Turf?

Artificial turf, also known as synthetic grass or fake grass, is a man-made surface designed to look like real grass. It is commonly made from synthetic fibers that are durable and require minimal maintenance. Artificial turf is commonly used in sports fields, playgrounds, and residential lawns as an alternative to natural grass.

It offers benefits such as water conservation, reduced need for pesticides and fertilizers, and a consistent green appearance throughout the year.

Environmental Benefits of Artificial Turf

Artificial turf offers several environmental benefits compared to natural grass. Here's why:

  • It requires significantly less water to maintain, saving gallons upon gallons of water annually.
  • Artificial turf does not need pesticides or fertilizers to stay lush, reducing harmful chemical runoff that can pollute water sources.
  • It eliminates the need for gasoline-powered lawnmowers, decreasing air pollution.
  • The durability of artificial turf means it does not require frequent replacement, cutting down on waste in landfills.
  • Its UV-resistant properties mean it won't fade or degrade quickly, leading to a longer lifespan and less material consumption overall.

Water Conservation

Artificial turf helps in saving water because it doesn't need regular watering like natural grass.

An artificial lawn can save approximately 55 gallons of water per square foot annually compared to a natural grass lawn.

This means you can significantly reduce your water usage by switching to artificial turf in your outdoor spaces — a meaningful impact both for your utility bill and for Oklahoma's water supply during dry summers.

Reduction in Chemical Usage

Using artificial turf significantly reduces the need for pesticides, fertilizers, and other chemicals that are commonly used to maintain natural grass. Artificial turf is virtually maintenance-free, requiring only occasional brushing and cleaning to keep it in good condition.

By eliminating the need for these chemicals, artificial turf helps promote a more environmentally friendly landscaping option — one that's safer for your kids, your pets, and your local watershed.

Artificial Turf and Air Quality

Installing artificial turf in your yard can actually improve air quality. Unlike natural grass, artificial turf does not require pesticides or fertilizers, which can release harmful chemicals into the air. Additionally, the maintenance of natural grass often involves gas-powered lawn mowers and trimmers, which contribute to air pollution.

Artificial turf eliminates the need for these machines, leading to cleaner air in your immediate surroundings.

Contribution to Biodiversity

Artificial turf contributes to biodiversity by providing a stable habitat for various insects and small animals. The ecosystem on artificial turf often includes ants, spiders, and other organisms that play a crucial role in maintaining balance in nature.

The different plant species used alongside artificial turf can attract pollinators like bees and butterflies, enhancing the overall biodiversity in the area. Additionally, reduced pesticide use means less disruption to the local wildlife.

Energy Savings

By installing artificial turf, you can save on energy costs since it requires no mowing, watering, or fertilizing. This leads to reduced energy consumption compared to maintaining natural grass lawns. The absence of mowing also reduces emissions from lawn mowers, contributing to environmental sustainability.

Durability and Longevity

Artificial turf lasts for many years without needing to be replaced. Unlike natural grass, it does not require water, fertilizers, or pesticides to stay lush and green. It can withstand heavy foot traffic and is resistant to fading, ensuring that your lawn looks vibrant for a long time — making it a cost-effective and environmentally sound choice in the long run.

Maintenance Practices

To keep your artificial turf looking fresh and vibrant, regular maintenance is still essential. Here are the key practices:

  • Regular Cleaning: Sweep debris like leaves, dirt, and twigs off the turf surface to prevent buildup and maintain its appearance.
  • Periodic Rinsing: Spray your turf with water occasionally to remove dust and pet waste, keeping it clean and odor-free.
  • Brushing: Use a brush or broom to fluff up the turf fibers and prevent matting, especially in high-traffic areas.
  • Applying Disinfectants: To maintain hygiene, apply mild disinfectants or cleaners designed specifically for artificial turf — like OxyTurf, which TOF carries and delivers to your door.

Conclusion

Artificial turf can have a significant positive environmental impact compared to natural grass. It requires less water, eliminates harmful pesticides and fertilizers, reduces air pollution from lawn equipment, and lasts for years without replacement. For Oklahoma homeowners looking to do right by their yard and the environment, synthetic turf is a compelling choice.
